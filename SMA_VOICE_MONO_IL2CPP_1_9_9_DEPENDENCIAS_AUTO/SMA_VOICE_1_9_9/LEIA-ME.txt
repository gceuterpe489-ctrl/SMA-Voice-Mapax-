ANTES DE INSTALAR / BEFORE INSTALLING
Leia o guia no seu idioma em manuals/. INSTALAR.bat baixa as dependencias ausentes.
Read the guide in your language in manuals/. INSTALAR.bat downloads missing game dependencies.
Extract the entire archive before running INSTALAR.bat.
Choose 1) SMA (Mono) or 2) CMA (IL2CPP), then the game root. No automatic game/backend selection.
INSTALAR_OLLAMA_E_MODELO.bat installs missing Ollama + qwen3:4b-instruct separately.
Existing dependencies and user models are preserved. CMA needs a first game launch to generate interop.
Optional exact ZIPs in dependencies/ are verified before installation.
Do not separate INSTALAR.bat from installer/.

SUPPORT / SUPORTE
After installation, two BATs are placed beside the game executable:
SMA_VERIFICAR.bat   -> SMA_VERIFICACAO.txt
SMA_DIAGNOSTICO.bat -> DIAGNOSTICO_SMA_VOICE.txt (option 2: UI snapshots)
Attach both TXT files + the complete BepInEx console/log to a GitHub Issue.
Review personal data before sharing. No automatic upload.

Dependencies and model weights are NOT included. See manuals/DEPENDENCY-VERSIONS.txt.
